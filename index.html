My Personal Story

I continued to Kanaba Secondary School and later St. Adolf Tibeyarilwa Muhorro Secondary School.

I pursued Information Technology at Kabale University and Uganda Martyrs University.

I continued developing my professional skills through BYU–Pathway Worldwide, Ensign College, Cisco Networking Academy and TestOut.

My work experience in data management, reporting, customer service and field operations has strengthened my professional abilities.
