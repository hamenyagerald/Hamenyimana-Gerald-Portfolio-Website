My Personal Story

From Kisoro to Information Technology

My educational journey began at Chibumba Primary School in Kisoro District, Uganda. I continued to Kanaba Secondary School and later St. Adolf Tibeyarilwa Muhorro Secondary School.

I pursued Information Technology at Kabale University and Uganda Martyrs University, and continued my professional development through BYU–Pathway Worldwide, Ensign College, Cisco Networking Academy and TestOut.

Alongside my studies, I have gained practical experience in data entry, customer relations, reporting and operational support.

These experiences have strengthened my interest in technology and motivated me to continue learning.
